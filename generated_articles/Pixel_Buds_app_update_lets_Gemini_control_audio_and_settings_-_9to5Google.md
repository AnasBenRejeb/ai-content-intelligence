It's a little more than a year since we first introduced you to the PiXeL, Google's first smart speaker, and a year since the company began testing out the device in the wild. The time has come, finally, for Google to launch the PiXeL 11.

The PiXeL 11 is the second generation of Google's smart speaker, and it has a few updates. The most significant change is the addition of a voice assistant. In the PiXeL 10, Google's in-house assistant was called "Google Assistant," and you could only ask questions via text message. The biggest addition here is the voice assistant, and if you've been following Google's smart speaker offerings, it's the biggest change.
