Google Contacts app is making it a little bit easier to find your own number on Android, with a new “Your info” card that mirrors longstanding functionality over on iPhone, while also just being a fun way to remind you that you have a phone number you can use with confidence.

You can use this card by swiping up from the bottom of your screen and tapping “Your info”, and the card will show you your phone number, along with some fun facts like your favorite song, the date you were born, and the year you’ll be 100.

It’s a great way to make sure you never forget your own phone number, especially if you have a few in your contacts. And if you ever forget it, all you have to do is swipe left and you’ll see the full phone number, including a GIF of a phone ringing.

But Google isn’t just making it easier to find your phone number on Android. For the first time on the app, the company is also allowing you to share your number with others, which might not be such a great idea if you don’t want anyone to know that you have a phone number.

You can share your number by tapping the “Share” button in the top right corner of the screen, and then tapping “Share with phone number”. This will prompt you to enter a message, along with your phone number. And when you’re done, you can tap “Done” to share.

This isn’t something you should do all the time. And even if you do it, you can’t delete the message. It just goes in a hidden folder somewhere.

The new sharing feature is available in Google Contacts 5.15, which is a new version of the app that was released today.
