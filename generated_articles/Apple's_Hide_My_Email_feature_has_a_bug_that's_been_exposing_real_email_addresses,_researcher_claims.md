# Apple's Hide My Email feature has a bug that's been exposing real email addresses, researcher claims

*Satirical Commentary | Original story from Unknown*

Apple's Hide My Email feature has a bug that's been exposing real email addresses.

Apple's Hide My Email feature is a convenient privacy tool that uses disposable addresses to hide a user’s true email for the sake of online anonymity. The feature was first introduced on iOS 13.4, but the bug was recently discovered by researchers at the University of California, Irvine.

The researchers discovered that the feature would store a user's email address for up to 30 minutes even if the app was deleted and reinstalled. This means that anyone who has an open email account could theoretically access the user's private email by simply running the app on a clean device.

The bug was not limited to just the iOS 13.4 version of the app. Researchers have discovered that the bug persisted on both the iOS 13.4 and the iOS 13.5 versions of the app, which were released on September 23rd, 2020.

The researchers have since patched the bug, but it's a reminder of the importance of using privacy-focused email services, even on the latest versions of the iOS operating system.

If you have an open email account, consider switching to a more secure email service like Gmail or Outlook.

For more information, check out the article by the researchers at the University of California, Irvine.
