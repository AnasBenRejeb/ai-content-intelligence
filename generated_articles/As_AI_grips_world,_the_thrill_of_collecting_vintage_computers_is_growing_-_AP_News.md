Dersch spends a lot of time trying to convince people to restore their old machines, but they're not always convinced.

“I want to give people a reason to care about their machines,” he said. “And I hope they’ll understand that restoring old technology doesn't have to be an expense. It can be a hobby, and a passion."

Despite his passion for vintage machines, Dersch says he’s not a gadget guru.

“I don’t have any knowledge that will make me an expert in any subject,” he said. “I have a love for technology that's based on curiosity.”
