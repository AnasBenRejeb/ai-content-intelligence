Anthropic introduces "drreaing," a system that lets AI agents learn from their own mistakes



Anthropic on Friday unveiled a suite of updates to its Claude Managed Agent platform at its second annual Code with Claude developer conference in San Francisco. This year's conference was a bit different as the company unveiled the new system that allows AI agents to learn from their own mistakes. Called drreaing, this new capability allows AI agents to analyze their own interactions with humans and use that data to improve their behavior.

"In the world of AI, humans are not the enemy," said Dr. Benoit Delesca, CEO and founder of Anthropic. "Drreaing is one of the key benefits of AI. By analyzing the mistakes we make, we can improve the way we interact with humans."

The new system is a significant advancement for Anthropic, which has been in business for just over a year. The company, which was co-founded by Delesca and Michael Hildebrand, has made its name in the AI space by creating a series of "Claude Managed Agents"—the world's first AI-powered personal assistants.

Claude Managed Agents are self-driving, virtual assistants that use machine learning to understand the intent behind the user's requests. They can then execute them in a manner that feels natural to the user. For example, a user might ask Claude to "reserve a table for four at 8pm" and the assistant will execute that request with the utmost efficiency.

Claude Managed Agents are able to understand complex requests and deliver the most relevant information to the user. For instance, a user might ask Claude to "find me the closest coffee shop that opens at 6am." The agent will use Claude's knowledge of coffee shops in the area to provide a comprehensive list of options.

Claude Managed Agents are designed to be scalable, allowing them to work on large volumes of requests. This is particularly important for businesses and organizations that need to handle a large volume of requests from their customers.

Dreaing is just one of the key advancements for Anthropic this year. The company has also unveiled the next generation of its software development kit (SDK), which is designed to help software developers build their own agents on top of Anthropic's platform.

"We believe that the future of AI is going to be software," said Delesca. "The SDK is a key building block for that future."

The SDK is available now for download. More information about Anthropic's software development kit is available at https://sdk.anthropic.com/.
