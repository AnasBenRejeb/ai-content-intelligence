# Google tests letting Gemini call businesses for you

*Satirical Commentary | Original story from Unknown*

Google, the search giant, is taking its AI-powered chatbots to a whole new level. The company has announced that it will soon let Gemini, the chatbot that’s created through an AI partnership with Meta, make calls on its behalf.

“Call for Me” is currently available to a few hundred businesses, but Google intends to expand the feature to millions of users by the end of this year.

The feature allows businesses to schedule appointments, book hotel rooms, and even order takeout food. Google says it uses AI to understand when it’s appropriate to make a call, and the chatbot can do a lot of the legwork on the phone, including scheduling appointments.

“We want to make sure businesses have a good experience with our search and search advertising, and the ability to connect directly through a phone number is a way to do that,” said Danny Sullivan, Google’s executive editor, Search and Display Products.

The move is a significant one for Google, which has been building out its AI capabilities over the years. The company has been experimenting with chatbots that can carry on conversations with businesses and customers, and the feature is an attempt to leverage that expertise to help businesses.

“We’re really excited about the opportunity to expand our reach to more businesses, and we believe this will be an excellent way to connect with them in a way that’s helpful to them,” said Sullivan.

The feature is still in beta, so businesses can still sign up to participate. Google will continue to update its chatbot’s abilities over time, Sullivan said, but for now it seems like the feature is meant for a specific set of businesses.

Google has also partnered with Gemini to build out its AI capabilities, which is a major part of Google’s efforts to keep up with Facebook and other tech giants that are leveraging AI.

“We’re building AI to understand what businesses are asking for, so we can provide those answers to people who need them,” said Sullivan. “It’s an important part of what we’re trying to do with the AI work we’re doing.”

Google also has plans to expand its AI capabilities beyond chatbots, which has been a big area of focus for the company. The company hopes to use AI to create its own virtual assistants, which it calls “intelligent agents,” in the future.

"We’re really focused on building AI into the fabric of Google so we can help people do things more easily and in more ways than before," said Sullivan. “We’re very excited about what the future holds for AI at Google.”
