Google Debuts Fitbit Edge, A Premium Fitness Band That Offers Continuous Tracking.

The world’s most popular fitness tracker has gone premium, as Google has introduced an updated premium fitness band with a screen, a device that’s a cross between a smartwatch and the more discert health gadgets that offer continuous tracking.

The $180 Fitbit Edge was announced at the Google I/O conference in Mountain View, California, on June 4, 2018, as the next-generation premium fitness tracker. The device features a 1.28-inch AMOLED touchscreen and can track metrics such as steps, calories burned, and heart rate.

Google is also introducing a new app that allows users to view their Fitbit data in real-time, in addition to the usual tracking features. The app will be available in both the Android and iOS app stores on July 11, 2018.

“We’ve had the best-selling Fitbit in the world for years. We wanted to build on that by creating something that’s even better — the Fitbit Edge,” said Eric Boulay, Fitbit senior vice president and head of global products.

The Fitbit Edge comes with a number of additional features, including a heart rate monitor, sleep tracker, and GPS to track runs and cycling.

The device also has an infrared sensor that can detect the user’s body temperature, which will be used to provide real-time feedback on the user’s temperature.

According to Google, the Fitbit Edge is the most advanced tracker the company has ever produced, and it’s the first Fitbit device that’s certified to offer continuous heart rate tracking.

The device is priced at $180.

In response to the news, Gizmodo’s Sam Rutherford said the Fitbit Edge is “a fitness band with a twist. It has a screen, it can track steps, it can track GPS, it can track sleep, it can track your heart rate, and it does it all using a display. It’s like the Google Pixel C, only a lot less expensive.”

Google has also unveiled a new app for the Fitbit Edge that allows users to see their data in real-time, as well as to receive notifications when they hit their daily step goals. The app will be available in both the Android and iOS app stores on July 11, 2018.

The Fitbit Edge will be available in the US, UK, and Canada on June 26, 2018.
