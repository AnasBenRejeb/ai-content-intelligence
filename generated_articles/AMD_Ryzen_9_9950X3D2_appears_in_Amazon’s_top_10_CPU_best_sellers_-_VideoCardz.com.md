AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D2 appeared in Amazon’s top 10 CPU best sellers - VideoCardz.com

AMD’s Ryzen 9 9950X3D2 has entered Amazon US’ top 10 CPU best-seller list, despite a review response that focused mostly on price and limited gains over cheaper X3D models. Reviewers did not question the superiority of the higher-end processor, but did note that the 9950X3D2 can be had for only $249.99, $50 less than the entry-level Ryzen 9 3950X.

The CPU boasts 16 Cores, 32 Threads, and 44MB of L3 Cache for a theoretical maximum of 1.8GHz. It has 256KB L2 Cache, but AMD says its L3 Cache is not counted in this figure, as it is not a L3 Cache.

The 9950X3D2 is the second-gen successor to the Ryzen 9 9900X, but with a lower base clock, which AMD says is due to its smaller die. AMD claims that the 9950X3D2 is a 3.1GHz clocked CPU, which is not as high as the 9900X’s 3.3GHz, but it is higher than the base clock of 3.0GHz for the Ryzen 7 3700X. The 9950X3D2 is available with up to 32GB of 2400MHz DDR4, or 16GB of 3200MHz DDR4.

A review of the CPU’s overclocking capabilities found that 256MHz overclocking of the CPU was possible, but the CPU would overclock only to 2.7GHz. The 9950X3D2 does not have a dedicated memory controller, but has an onboard 10GbE controller, which is useful for gaming and other demanding applications.

The Ryzen 9 9950X3D2 is the second CPU in the 3D series, after the Ryzen 9 3D50X3D1. AMD has released a pair of 3D CPUs in the past, the Ryzen 9 3D50X and 3D50X-M.

The 9950X3D2 is available now from Amazon for $249.99.

The 9900X3D2 is not yet available.
