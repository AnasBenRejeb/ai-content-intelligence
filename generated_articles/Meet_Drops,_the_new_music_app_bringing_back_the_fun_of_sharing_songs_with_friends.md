Meet Drop, the new music app bringing back the fun of sharing songs with friends.

“Drop's success lies in our dedication to providing a platform where friends can share music and connect in real-time,” said Drop's CEO, Adam Wong. 

With Drop's free and paid options, users can enjoy the app's premium features, including:

- Unlimited song streams
- In-app purchases for premium playlists and beats
- Discover and create playlists
- Multi-device syncing
- Chat with friends in-app

Drop's free plan is available on the App Store and Google Play for $1.99/month, while its premium plan is $4.99/month. 

“Drop is a game-changer in the music discovery industry, offering users an easy-to-use app with unmatched features and an amazing community," said Drop's marketing director, Evan Kwon. 

Drop is available now on the App Store and Google Play, and will be available on Android in the coming weeks.
